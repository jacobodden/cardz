Pay Me

An 11-round Contract Rummy game for 2 to 6 players. Lowest score wins.

Description

Players build melds to empty their hands. Hand sizes grow by one card each round, from 3 to 13 cards, and the wild card changes every round to match the number of cards in play.

Setup

Deck

Use one standard deck for 2 players, two decks for 3–4, or three decks for 5–6. Jokers are omitted.

Table

Place the remaining deck in the center as a draw pile and flip the top card face up to start the discard pile.

Wilds

All cards matching the round's hand size are wild. Round 1 deals 3 cards, so threes are wild; round 11 deals 13 cards, so kings are wild.

Gameplay

Dealing

The first hand deals 3 cards to each player. Each later round adds 1 more card, peaking at 13 cards in round 11.

Turns

On your turn, draw either the top face-up card from the discard pile or the top card from the stock pile.

Melding

If you have valid melds (a set of 3 or more of a kind, or a run of 3 or more consecutive cards in the same suit), you can lay them on the table.

Discarding

End your turn by discarding one card face up.

Going out

When you can lay down your entire hand, say “Pay Me”. Every other player may draw one more card to try to finish melding their hand. You then lay down your melded hand, and the other players may play off it with any unmelded cards in their own hand, for example:

  • adding another 4 to your set of fours;
  • adding the 9♣ to your run of 5♣ 6♣ 7♣ 8♣ (it must match the suit).

Scoring

Each player scores the value of the cards left in their hand:

CardPoints
Jack, Queen, King10
Ace1
2–10Face value

The player with the lowest total after all 11 rounds wins the pot.
